# Migration from Slack to Microsoft Teams: Important Details

As we continue to evolve and improve our internal communications, we understand that change can be challenging. We acknowledge the efforts you've made to adapt to our previous adjustments and appreciate your flexibility. However, to better align with our growing needs and to enhance our collaboration tools, we will be migrating from Slack to Microsoft Teams starting Q3.

## Frequently Asked Questions
1. **Why are we migrating from Slack to Microsoft Teams?**
We are migrating to enhance our collaboration capabilities, integrate better with our existing Microsoft suite, and improve security features.
2. **How will this migration affect my daily work?**
You will notice changes in how you communicate and collaborate with your team. Microsoft Teams offers similar functionalities to Slack but with a more integrated approach to our Microsoft environment.
3. **What support will be available during the migration?**
Our IT department will be available to assist with any issues or questions you may have. You can reach out to them via [support@company.com](mailto:support@company.com).

## Employee Action Checklist
* Review Microsoft Teams tutorials and guides provided by the company
* Update your Microsoft Teams app to the latest version
* Familiarize yourself with Microsoft Teams' interface and features
* Participate in departmental training sessions
* Update your contacts and groups in Microsoft Teams
* Test Microsoft Teams with your team
* Report any issues to the IT department
* Attend a Q&A session with the IT department
* Review and understand the new communication protocols
* Provide feedback on the migration process
