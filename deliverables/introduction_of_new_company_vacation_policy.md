# Introduction of New Company Vacation Policy

We are excited to announce the introduction of a new vacation policy, designed to provide our employees with more flexibility and autonomy in managing their time off. As of the start of next quarter, we will be transitioning to an unlimited PTO model, which will replace our current policy.

## Key Highlights of the New Policy
* **Unlimited PTO**: Employees will no longer be limited to a set number of paid time off days per year.
* **Effective Date**: The new policy will take effect at the start of next quarter.
* **Manager Approval**: While the new policy offers more flexibility, manager approval is still required for all time off requests.
* **Minimum Encouraged Time Off**: We encourage all employees to take a minimum of 10 days off per year to recharge and relax.
* **No Unused PTO Payout**: Under the new policy, unused PTO will not be paid out at the end of the year.

## Comparison of Old and New Policies
|  | Old Policy | New Policy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PTO Days | Limited to 15 days per year | Unlimited |
| Manager Approval | Required | Required |
| Minimum Time Off | Not specified | 10 days per year encouraged |
| Unused PTO Payout | Paid out at the end of the year | Not paid out |

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Q: How do I request time off under the new policy?
A: You can request time off by submitting a request to your manager, who will review and approve or deny it.
### Q: Can I carry over unused PTO to the next year?
A: No, under the new policy, unused PTO will not be carried over to the next year.
### Q: How will the new policy affect my benefits?
A: The new policy will not affect your benefits, but it will provide you with more flexibility in managing your time off.

We believe that this new policy will have a positive impact on our employees' work-life balance and overall well-being. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to your manager or HR representative.
