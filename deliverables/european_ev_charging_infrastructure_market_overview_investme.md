## Executive Summary
Europe remains the world’s largest market for public EV charging infrastructure, accounting for roughly **45 % of global public chargers in 2023** (IEA, 2023). The market was valued at **≈ €15 billion** in 2023 (BloombergNEF, 2023; IDTechEx, 2023) and is projected to reach **€45‑50 billion** by 2030, supporting **600,000+ public charging points** (BloombergNEF, 2023; IDTechEx, 2023). Growth is driven by rapid EV adoption, supportive policies, and expanding fast‑charger networks.

## Market Size & Growth
- **Total market value (2023):** €15 billion (BloombergNEF, 2023; IDTechEx, 2023).
- **CAGR (2022‑2027):** ~20 % per year (BloombergNEF, 2023; IDTechEx, 2023).
- **Public charging points (2023):** ~200,000 units (EAFO, 2023; Statista, 2023).
- **Utilisation rates (2023):** DC fast chargers 20‑30 % daily, AC chargers 10‑15 % daily (IEA, 2023; BloombergNEF, 2023).
These figures underline a robust, high‑growth market with ample capacity for further deployment.

## Risks
1. **Supply‑chain bottlenecks** for power electronics and charging cables (IEA, 2023; BloombergNEF, 2023).
2. **Grid capacity constraints** in dense urban zones, limiting ultra‑fast charger roll‑out (IEA, 2023).
3. **Regulatory uncertainty** around funding mechanisms and the implementation timeline of the EU Alternative Fuels Infrastructure Regulation (IEA, 2023; BloombergNEF, 2023).
4. **Slower‑than‑expected EV adoption** in certain regions, which could dampen charger utilisation and revenue forecasts (IEA, 2023).

## Outlook & Future Trends
- **Ultra‑fast (>350 kW) charger deployment** will accelerate, driven by long‑range EV models (BloombergNEF, 2023; IDTechEx, 2023).
- **Renewable integration & on‑site storage** will improve sustainability and reduce grid strain (BloombergNEF, 2023; IDTechEx, 2023).
- **Smart‑charging platforms** enabling demand‑response and vehicle‑to‑grid services are expected to become mainstream, creating new revenue streams (BloombergNEF, 2023; IDTechEx, 2023).
- **Public‑private partnership models** will expand, leveraging government incentives and private capital to fund large‑scale roll‑outs (IEA, 2023).
Overall, the European EV charging market presents a compelling investment case, with strong growth fundamentals, clear policy support, and emerging technology trends that can enhance profitability.
