## Six Practical Steps to Get Started

1. **Identify high‑volume, low‑complexity queries** – e.g., store hours, order status, pricing.
2. **Pick a plug‑and‑play platform** – ManyChat, Tidio, or a ChatGPT wrapper with a free tier.
3. **Use pre‑built templates** to draft conversation flows; tweak the language to match your brand.
4. **Run a pilot** with internal staff or a small group of loyal customers; collect feedback.
5. **Launch publicly** on the channels where your customers already chat (website widget, Facebook Messenger, WhatsApp).
6. **Monitor metrics** (response time, CSAT, cost per interaction) and iterate every 2‑4 weeks.

This roadmap keeps technical overhead minimal while delivering quick wins【Practical rollout steps for minimal overhead: (1) identify high‑volume, low‑complexity queries; (2) select a plug‑and‑play platform; (3) use pre‑built templates to draft conversation flows; (4) run a pilot with internal staff; (5) launch publicly; (6) monitor metrics and iterate.】.
